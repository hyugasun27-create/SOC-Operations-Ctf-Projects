## Evidence handling

- Never execute the suspicious attachment or downloaded payload.
- Preserve original artifacts and analyze working copies.
- Calculate SHA-256 before analysis.
- Do not publish memory dumps, malware binaries, credentials, or private data.
- Redact sensitive information from screenshots.
